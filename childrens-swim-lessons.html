Why start early

A developmental head start

The Griffith University study confirmed that children who take early swim lessons put them at a developmental advantage compared to peers who do not take swim lessons.

Did You Know? According to a 2020 study, the younger the age at which children started swim lessons, the earlier the child was able to attain aquatic competencies within their developmental capabilities.

A life saving skill they keep forever

In addition to giving your child a physical activity, they will acquire a LIFE SAVING SKILL that they will have forever have one day after receiving lessons.

Here are some real statistics that expose the scary side to playing in the pool:

USA Swimming Foundation states "drowning remains the leading cause of unintentional death for children ages one to four"

In a study run by the National Institute of Child Health and Human Development found children who participated in formal swim lessons had an 88% reduced risk of drowning in ages 1-4 years old!
